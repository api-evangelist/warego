# WareGo: Best WMS Software for 3PL, Retail, Manufacturing & More

> WareGo is a cloud-based Warehouse Management System (WMS) designed for wholesalers, distributors, 3PLs, and ecommerce businesses. It helps companies manage inventory, streamline warehouse operations, track serial numbers, and automate order fulfillment.

WareGo serves industries including wholesale distribution, ecommerce, retail, cold storage, agriculture, and 3PL providers. The software is available as a SaaS platform with pricing based on business size and feature requirements.
